A Different Kind of Lending Platform

Built on trust, transparency, and community support

Emergency Consumption Only

We only facilitate emergency consumption loans, not business or investment loans.

Friends Lend to Friends

Lending happens only within trusted groups where people know each other.

Subscription-Based Revenue

Platform earns only from lender subscriptions, not from loan commissions.

Strong Reputation System

Comprehensive rating and blacklist enforcement for accountability.

No Cross-Border Lending

Lending never crosses country borders or group boundaries.

No Money Custody

M-Pesewa never holds money. All transactions happen outside the platform.

How It Works

Simple, transparent process for emergency lending

1

Join Your Country

Select your country to join the M-Pesewa ecosystem. Each country operates independently.

2

Join or Create a Group

Join an existing trusted group or create your own with friends, family, or community.

3

Borrowers Post Requests

Borrowers post emergency loan requests with category, amount, and purpose.

4

Lenders Offer Terms

Lenders in the same group review requests and offer lending terms.

5

Money Sent Outside Platform

Once terms are agreed, money is sent directly between users outside M-Pesewa.

6

Ledger & Reputation Tracking

M-Pesewa tracks the ledger and updates reputation scores for both parties.

7

Repayment & Closure

Borrower repays within 7 days. Loan is marked as cleared in the ledger.

M-Pesewa does not hold money. Ever.

Risk Disclaimer

Important Notice

All lending involves risk. M-Pesewa provides no guarantees of repayment. Default risk is managed through group enforcement, reputation systems, and blacklisting. Lenders must conduct their own due diligence within their trusted groups.

The platform's role is limited to facilitating connections, tracking agreements, and maintaining reputation systems. All financial transactions occur directly between users outside the platform.
