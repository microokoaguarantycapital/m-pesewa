How M-Pesewa Works

Simple, transparent, and built on trust

1

Select Country & Register

Choose your country, provide basic details, and select your role as borrower or lender

2

Join or Create a Group

Connect with trusted circles through referral-only groups (family, church, professional)

3

Shop Categories & Add to Cart

Select from 20 emergency categories. Borrowers add requests to group cart, lenders review

4

Lend & Repay with Trust

Lenders select from cart, disburse off-platform. Repay in 7 days with 10% interest

👤 For Borrowers

  • No need to ask for help or favors
  • Post requests and receive offers based on trust rating
  • Borrow faster from people who know and trust you
  • No favors owed - transparent and voluntary
  • Fair interest rates agreed by friends and family
  • One request reaches many lenders in your group
  • Fast, easy, and affordable emergency money

💼 For Lenders

  • Earn passive income from day one
  • Lend confidently based on borrower ratings
  • Start with as little as $0.10
  • No pressure to lend - choose who to support
  • Transparent interest rates you're comfortable with
  • Support people you trust while earning returns
  • Grow at your own pace
