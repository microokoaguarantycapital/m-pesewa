How M-Pesewa Works

Four simple steps to emergency financial support

1

Select Your Country

Choose from 13 African countries. All activities are country-locked for trust and compliance.

2

Join or Create a Group

Connect with trusted circles - family, church, professional networks, or local communities.

3

Choose Your Role

Register as Borrower (free) or Lender (subscription required). You can have both roles separately.

4

Start Lending or Borrowing

Borrowers post requests, Lenders select borrowers. All transactions happen off-platform.
