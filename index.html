How M-pesewa Works

1

Join a Trusted Group

Get invited to a group in your country. Groups are family, church, professional, or local circles.

2

Choose Your Role

Register as a Borrower (free) or Lender (subscription required). You can have both roles with separate profiles.

3

Request or Offer Loans

Borrowers request loans for specific needs. Lenders in the same group can offer loans based on trust.

4

Track & Repay

Ledgers track all loans. Repay within 7 days with 10% interest. Daily partial repayments allowed.

👤 For Borrowers

  • No need to ask for help or favors – Post your loan request in your group.
  • Borrow faster from people who already know and trust you.
  • No favors owed – lending is transparent and voluntary.
  • Fair interest rates agreed willingly by friends and family.
  • One request reaches many lenders when shared within the group.
  • Money when you need it – fast, easy, and affordable.

💼 For Lenders

  • Earn passive income from day one.
  • Lend confidently based on borrower ratings and group trust.
  • Start lending with as little as 0.1$.
  • No pressure to lend – you choose who to support.
  • Transparent interest rates you're comfortable with.
  • Support people you trust while earning returns.
