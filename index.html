How It Works

1

Join your country

Select your country from our supported African nations. This locks your currency and local groups.

2

Join or create a trusted group

Connect with friends, family, church members, or professional colleagues in a private group.

3

Borrowers post requests

Need emergency help? Post your request with amount, category, and repayment plan.

4

Trust & Safety

What M-Pesewa Is

  • A technology coordination platform – We connect borrowers and lenders within trusted groups
  • A record-keeping system – We maintain ledgers, track repayments, and manage reputations
  • A subscription service for lenders – We charge lenders for access to our platform
  • A reputation enforcement system – We blacklist defaulters platform-wide

What M-Pesewa Is NOT

  • Not a bank – We don't accept deposits or provide banking services
  • Not a lender – We don't lend money to anyone
  • Not a borrower – We don't borrow money from anyone
  • Not holding money – We never custody user funds
  • Not a debt collector – We don't participate in loan recovery

Risk Disclaimer

Lending has risk: There is no guarantee of repayment. Defaults can and do happen.
No platform guarantees: M-Pesewa does not guarantee any loan or repayment.
Group enforcement only: Recovery depends on social pressure within groups.

Platform Objectives

  • Emergency access to small amounts for real needs
  • Reduce predatory lending in communities

Target Users

  • Informal & professional lenders
  • Churches, families, social groups
  • Community lenders with idle cash
  • People who want to help friends safely
  • Those excluded from formal banking
