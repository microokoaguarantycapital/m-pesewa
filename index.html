How It Works

Four simple steps to get emergency support or earn returns.

1

Select Country & Register

Choose your country, provide your details and two guarantors. Start as a borrower (free) or lender (subscription required).

2

Join or Create a Group

Get invited to a trusted group (family, church, professional) or start your own. Groups are country-locked and referral-only.

3

Shop & Add to Group Cart

Borrowers select from 20 emergency categories, add loan requests to the group cart. Lenders do not see individual borrowers.

4

Lenders Select & Disburse

Lenders with active subscriptions view the cart, check borrower profiles, and select loans to fund. All money moves off-platform.
