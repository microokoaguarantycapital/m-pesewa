Trust & Safety

Platform Role

  • ❌ Not a bank
  • ❌ Not a lender
  • ❌ Not a borrower
  • ❌ Not holding money
  • ✅ Technology coordination platform
  • ✅ Record-keeping system
  • ✅ Reputation enforcement

Risk Disclaimer

⚠️ Important: Lending has risk.

  • No platform guarantees on repayment
  • All loans are private agreements between users
  • Group enforcement is primary mechanism
  • Blacklist system for defaults > 2 months
  • Debt collection is borrower/lender responsibility

M-Pesewa provides tools but cannot guarantee loan performance.
