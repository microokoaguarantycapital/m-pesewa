  • ✓ Provide emergency access to small consumption loans
  • ✓ Reduce reliance on predatory lending in communities
  • ✓ Monetize through lender subscriptions only
  • ✓ Enforce strict hierarchy & isolation rules
  • ✓ Build trust through group-based accountability

Individuals with emergency needs Informal & professional lenders Churches, families, social groups Community lenders Small business associations
