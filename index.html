How M-Pesewa Works

Four simple steps to get emergency support or earn returns

1
🌍

Select Country & Register

Choose your country, register with your details and 2 guarantors. Select your role as Borrower or Lender.

2
👥

Join or Create a Group

Get invited to a trusted group or create your own. Minimum 5 members, maximum 1000 per group.

3
🛒

Shop Categories & Add to Cart

Borrowers select emergency categories and add loan requests to the group cart. Lenders view available requests.

4
🤝

Lend, Disburse & Repay

Lenders select borrowers, disburse off-platform, and track repayments. 10% interest weekly, 5% daily penalty after 7 days.
