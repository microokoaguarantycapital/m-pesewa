How M-Pesewa Works

A 4-step process to get emergency support from trusted people

1

Select Country & Register

Choose your country from 13 African nations. Register with your name, phone, and 2 guarantors who know you well.

2

Join or Create a Trusted Group

Join a group by invitation from someone you trust, or create your own. Groups are country-locked with 5-1000 members.

3

Shop Categories & Add to Cart

Select from 20 emergency categories. Add your loan request to your group cart. Lenders in your group will see your request.

4

Lenders Select & Disburse Off-Platform

Lenders in your group select from the cart, check ratings, and disburse directly via M-Pesa, bank, or cash. Repay in 7 days with 10% interest.
