How M-Pesewa Works

Simple, transparent, and built on trust

1
📍

Select Country & Register

Choose your country, register with basic details, and provide 2 guarantors from your community.

2
👥

Join or Create a Group

Join existing trusted groups or create your own. Groups are referral-only with 5-1000 members.

3
🛒

Shop Loan Categories

Browse emergency loan categories and add to your group cart. Lenders in your group see your request.

4
💰

Lenders Fund & Track

Lenders choose who to support, disburse funds off-platform, and track repayments with 10% interest.

🤝

No More Favors

Borrow Faster

📈

Transparent Rates

One Request, Many Lenders
