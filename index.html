Trust & Safety

Platform Role

  • ❌ Not a bank
  • ❌ Not a lender
  • ❌ Not a borrower
  • ❌ Not holding money
  • ✅ Technology coordination platform
  • ✅ Record-keeping system

Risk Disclaimer

⚠️ Important: Lending has inherent risks.

  • No platform guarantees on repayment
  • All loans are private agreements
