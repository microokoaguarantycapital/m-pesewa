General Questions

What is M-PESEWA?

▼

M-PESEWA is a technology platform that enables emergency micro-lending within trusted groups across Africa. We are NOT a bank, NOT a lender, NOT a borrower, and we NEVER hold money. We provide the technology infrastructure for friends, families, and communities to support each other with small emergency loans transparently and safely.

Who holds the money?

▼

NOT M-PESEWA. All money transfers happen directly between users outside our platform via their preferred method (mobile money, bank transfer, cash, etc.). We only provide record-keeping and coordination services. We never touch, hold, or control any funds.

How does M-PESEWA make money?

▼

We charge subscription fees to lenders only. Borrowers use the platform for FREE. We have 4 subscription tiers (Basic, Premium, Super, Lender of Lenders) that provide different lending limits and features. We do NOT charge commissions on loans or take any percentage of loan amounts.

Is M-PESEWA regulated?

▼

M-PESEWA operates as a technology coordination and record-keeping platform, not as a financial institution. We comply with all relevant technology and data protection regulations in each country we operate in. Since we don't handle money, we don't require banking licenses. All loans are private agreements between users.

Borrowing Questions

How much can I borrow?

▼

Loan amounts depend on your group, your rating, and what lenders in your group are willing to offer. There's no fixed maximum, but typical emergency loans range from $10 to $500 in local currency. Remember: these are for EMERGENCY needs, not large amounts.

What is the repayment period?

▼

Standard loan tenure is 7 days. After 7 days, a 5% daily penalty applies to the outstanding balance. After 2 months (60 days) of non-payment, the loan is considered in default and the borrower is added to the global blacklist.

What interest rate do I pay?

▼

The standard interest rate is 10% for 7 days. However, interest rates are agreed willingly between borrower and lender. The platform suggests 10% as a fair rate, but you can negotiate different terms with your lender.

Can I be both a borrower and lender?

▼

Yes, but you need separate accounts. You must register once as a borrower and once as a lender. You cannot use the same account for both roles. When switching roles, you must log out and log into the appropriate account.

How many groups can I join?

▼

Borrowers can join up to 4 groups, but only with good ratings (4+ stars). New borrowers start with 1 group and can add more as their rating improves. This limit prevents over-borrowing and ensures responsible borrowing behavior.

Lending Questions

Do I need a subscription to lend?

▼

YES. All lenders must have an active subscription. We offer 4 tiers: Basic (KES 50/month), Premium (KES 250/month), Super (KES 1,000/month), and Lender of Lenders (KES 500/month). Each tier has different weekly limits and features. Subscription expires on the 28th of each month.

What happens if my subscription expires?

▼

Your lending access is immediately blocked. You can view your ledgers but cannot create new loans or interact with borrower requests. To restore access, you must renew your subscription. There's a 3-day grace period, after which your account is temporarily suspended.

Can I lend to anyone?

▼

NO. You can only lend to borrowers within your own groups. This ensures lending happens between people who know and trust each other. There's NO cross-group lending and NO cross-country lending.

What if a borrower doesn't repay?

▼

After 7 days, 5% daily penalty applies. After 2 months, borrower is blacklisted platform-wide. You can engage debt collectors (see our directory) at your own cost. M-PESEWA does not guarantee repayment - lending has inherent risk. This is why lending within trusted groups is crucial.

What is a ledger?

▼

A ledger is a private accounting book that tracks a specific loan. When you approve a loan request, a ledger is automatically created under your account. It shows borrower details, loan terms, repayment schedule, and payment history. You can have unlimited ledgers.

Groups Questions

What is a group?

▼

A group is a trusted circle of people who know each other - like family, church members, colleagues, or community members. Groups have 5-1,000 members, one admin/founder, and are country-locked. All lending happens ONLY within groups.

How do I join a group?

▼

Groups are invitation or referral only. You must be invited by a group member or referred by someone they trust. This ensures groups remain trusted circles. You can request an invitation through the platform, but acceptance depends on group approval.

Can I create my own group?

▼

Yes! You can create a group if you can mobilize at least 5 initial members. As the founder/admin, you'll invite the first members, set group rules, and moderate activity. Groups can be family groups, church groups, professional groups, etc.

Can groups have their own rules?

▼

Yes! While the platform has core rules (7-day tenure, 10% interest, etc.), groups can set additional internal rules like minimum/maximum amounts, preferred loan categories, or special terms for members. The group admin enforces these rules.

Subscription Questions

Why 4 subscription tiers?

▼

Different lenders have different capacities. Basic is for beginners (KES 1,500/week limit). Premium is for active lenders (KES 5,000/week). Super is for serious lenders (KES 20,000/week, CRB check required). Lender of Lenders is for professionals (KES 50,000/week, 30-day minimum tenure).

Why expire on the 28th?

▼

The 28th aligns with typical month-end cycles in African markets. It gives lenders predictable billing and ensures platform revenue stability. You receive reminders on the 25th, 26th, and 27th. There's a 3-day grace period after the 28th.

Can I upgrade/downgrade?

▼

Yes, you can change tiers at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Prorated refunds are not available. If you downgrade, ensure your active loans don't exceed your new tier's limits.

What payment methods do you accept?

▼

We accept mobile money (M-Pesa, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, etc.), bank transfers, and credit/debit cards. Payment processing is handled by third-party providers. Once payment is confirmed, your subscription is activated immediately.

Safety & Security Questions

How does the blacklist work?

▼

Default after 2 months → automatic blacklist. Blacklisted borrowers cannot borrow anywhere on the platform and cannot join new groups. Removal requires: 1) Full repayment (principal + interest + penalties), 2) Admin approval. Blacklist is platform-wide, not just country or group.

What is the 5-star rating system?

▼

Lenders rate borrowers after each loan (1-5 stars). Ratings affect: 1) How many groups you can join (max 4 requires 4+ stars), 2) Your borrowing limits, 3) Lenders' willingness to fund you. Ratings are visible to lenders in your groups.

What about data privacy?

▼

We follow strict data protection principles. Your personal data is encrypted and only shared with group members. We never sell your data. Full details in our Privacy Policy. You control what information is visible to others in your groups.

How do you prevent fraud?

▼

Multiple layers: 1) Group-based trust (you know who you're dealing with), 2) Country isolation (no cross-border fraud), 3) Rating system, 4) Blacklist system, 5) Manual verification for large loans, 6) Transaction monitoring for suspicious patterns.
