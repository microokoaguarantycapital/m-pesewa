Platform Roadmap

Our journey and future plans for building Africa's most trusted emergency lending platform

🚀 Our Vision Forward

M-Pesewa is on a mission to transform emergency lending across Africa. This roadmap outlines where we've been, where we are today, and where we're going. We believe in transparency and want our community to be part of our journey.

Completed
2016

Platform Foundation

Initial concept development and core architecture design. Focus on trust-based lending model for African communities.

Research Architecture

Beta Launch - Kenya

Limited beta launch in Kenya with 50 pilot groups. Tested core features: group creation, lending, reputation system.

Beta Kenya
2020

Public Launch & Expansion

Full public launch in Kenya. Introduced subscription tiers, blacklist system, and expanded to Uganda and Tanzania.

Launch Expansion
