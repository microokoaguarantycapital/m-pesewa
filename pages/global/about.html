About M-Pesewa

Revolutionizing emergency micro-lending in Africa through trusted circles

🎯

Our Mission

To provide immediate, accessible, and trustworthy emergency micro-lending solutions that empower African communities to overcome financial challenges within their trusted networks.

👁️

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted emergency lending platform, connecting millions through responsible lending circles that foster financial inclusion and community resilience.

🤝

Our Values

Trust, Transparency, Community, Accountability, and Financial Empowerment form the core of everything we do at M-Pesewa.

Core Philosophy

Emergency Consumption Loans Only

We focus exclusively on emergency consumption needs - not business capital, not investment, not luxury. When you need fare to work, data to complete a gig, gas to cook dinner, medicine for a sick child.

Friends Lend to Friends

All lending happens within trusted groups. Your church members, family network, professional colleagues, or local community. People who know you, trust you, and want to see you succeed.

Platform Earns Only from Lender Subscriptions

Borrowers pay nothing to use M-Pesewa. We earn revenue only from lenders who choose to subscribe for enhanced features and higher lending limits. No hidden fees, no percentage cuts from loans.

Strong Reputation & Blacklist Enforcement

Our 5-star rating system and strict blacklist policies ensure that trustworthy borrowers get priority access, while defaulters face platform-wide consequences until they make things right.

No Cross-Country or Cross-Group Lending

Strict hierarchy enforcement: Global → Country → Groups → Lenders → Borrowers. This maintains trust, accountability, and cultural relevance within each community.
