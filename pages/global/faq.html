Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about M-Pesewa emergency lending

General Questions

M-Pesewa is a technology platform that enables emergency micro-lending within trusted circles. We are NOT a bank, NOT a lender, NOT a borrower, and do NOT hold user funds. We provide the infrastructure for friends, family, and community members to lend to each other with proper tracking, reputation systems, and structured terms.

  • Trust-based: Lending happens within existing trusted relationships, not algorithms
  • No fund handling: We don't hold or transfer money - all transactions happen directly between users
  • Emergency-focused: Specific 20 categories for genuine emergencies only
  • Community-driven: Group accountability instead of collateral requirements
  • Fair pricing: Transparent 10% interest with no hidden fees
  • Dual roles: Users can be both borrowers and lenders

We currently operate in 12 Sub-Saharan African countries:

🇰🇪 Kenya (KSh) 🇺🇬 Uganda (UGX) 🇹🇿 Tanzania (TZS) 🇷🇼 Rwanda (RWF) 🇧🇮 Burundi (BIF) 🇨🇩 DRC (USD/CDF) 🇸🇸 South Sudan (SSP) 🇿🇦 South Africa (ZAR) 🇳🇬 Nigeria (NGN) 🇬🇭 Ghana (GHS) 🇪🇹 Ethiopia (ETB) 🇸🇴 Somalia (SOS)

M-Pesewa operates as a technology platform, not a financial institution. Since we don't handle user funds, we don't require banking licenses. However, we comply with all local data protection and consumer protection laws in each country we operate. Our terms and conditions are legally binding and designed to protect all parties involved.

Only from lender subscriptions. Borrowers pay nothing to use the platform. Lenders pay a monthly, bi-annual, or annual subscription fee based on their chosen tier (Basic, Premium, Super, or Lender of Lenders). We don't take commissions or percentages from loans - all interest goes directly to lenders.

Our platform provides several dispute resolution mechanisms:

  1. Group Admin mediation: The group founder/admin can help resolve issues
  2. Reputation system: Both parties can rate each other
  3. Blacklist system: Serious defaulters get platform-wide visibility
  4. Debt collectors: We provide access to vetted debt collection services
  5. Platform Admin: Final arbitration for unresolved cases

However, we encourage users to resolve disputes amicably within their trusted circles first.

Borrower Questions

Borrowing limits depend on your lender's subscription tier:

  • From Basic lenders: Up to 1,500 local currency
  • From Premium lenders: Up to 5,000 local currency
  • From Super lenders: Up to 20,000 local currency
  • From Lender of Lenders: Up to 50,000 local currency

Minimum loan amount is as low as 5 KSh (or equivalent in local currency).

  • Interest rate: 10% per loan (fixed)
  • Repayment period: 7 days (1 week)
  • Partial repayments: Allowed (daily if needed)
  • Penalty after 7 days: 5% daily on outstanding balance
  • Default: After 2 months of non-payment
  • One active loan: Only one loan per group at a time

You can join a group through:

  1. Invitation: Someone in the group must invite you
  2. Referral: Two referrers from the same group must vouch for you
  3. Group search: Browse public groups and request to join (requires approval)

Important: You can join up to 4 groups maximum, and only if you have a good rating (4+ stars).

If you can't repay on time:

  1. Communicate early: Contact your lender immediately
  2. Partial payments: Make whatever payment you can to reduce penalty
  3. Penalty applies: 5% daily on outstanding balance after day 7
  4. Rating impact: Your borrower rating will be affected
  5. Group migration: You can borrow from another group to repay (if you have multiple groups)
  6. Blacklist risk: After 2 months, you risk being blacklisted platform-wide

Yes! This is one of our key innovations. You can:

  • Have separate profiles as borrower and lender
  • Borrow in one group and lend in another
  • Switch between roles as needed
  • Build reputation in both roles independently

To change roles, you need to log out and start a new registration for the other role.

The blacklist system protects lenders from chronic defaulters:

  • Trigger: 2 months of non-payment
  • Consequences: Cannot borrow, cannot join new groups
  • Visibility: Blacklist badge visible platform-wide
  • Removal: Only by Platform Admin after full repayment (principal + interest + penalties)
  • Defaulters Registry: Public list of blacklisted individuals

Subscription expiry date: 28th of each month

  • All subscriptions expire on the 28th, regardless of signup date
  • You'll see "Days remaining before expiry" on your dashboard
  • If subscription expires, lending access is blocked until payment
  • You can still view your portfolio and history when expired
  • Payment confirmation unlocks platform immediately

The ledger is automatically created when you approve a loan:

  • One ledger per borrower (named using borrower name)
  • Unlimited ledgers per lender
  • Auto-calculates: Interest (10%), penalties (5% daily after 7 days)
  • Tracks: Dates, amounts, status (Active/Cleared)
  • Manual updates: You update repayments as they come
  • Admin override: Platform Admin can correct if needed

Use our 5-star rating system after loan completion:

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐

  • 4 stars: Good - minor delays but good communication
  • 3 stars: Average - delays with some communication
  • 2 stars: Poor - significant delays, poor communication
  • 1 star: Very poor - defaulted, blacklist consideration

Good ratings unlock more group access for borrowers (up to 4 groups).

Group Questions

  1. Register as a lender (subscription required for group creation)
  2. Go to Groups → Create New Group
  3. Choose group name, type (Family, Church, Professional, etc.)
  4. Set group rules and description
  5. Invite first 5 members (minimum requirement)
  6. Activate group after members join

Note: As Founder/Admin, you moderate the group and invite members.

  • Minimum: 5 members (to activate group)
  • Maximum: 1,000 members per group
  • Unlimited groups: Per country, per user
  • Member mix: Can have both lenders and borrowers
  • Growth: Members can invite more members

Yes, with limits:

  • Borrowers: Maximum 4 groups (with good rating)
  • Lenders: Unlimited groups (subscription applies to all)
  • Group migration: Can move between groups with good record
  • Cross-group lending: Not allowed - lend only within each group

This allows borrowers to get loans from multiple sources while maintaining trust boundaries.

If the group Founder/Admin leaves:

  1. Admin transfer: Can transfer admin rights to another member
  2. Platform Admin: Can appoint new admin if needed
  3. Group continues: Loans and ledgers remain active
  4. New invitations: New admin can invite members
  5. If no admin: Group becomes read-only until admin appointed

Yes, groups can define internal rules:

  • Meeting frequency: Physical or virtual meetings
  • Member requirements: Additional criteria beyond platform minimums
  • Loan approval process: Group voting or admin decision
  • Social rules: Conduct expectations
  • Penalty rules: Additional consequences for default

These rules must not conflict with platform-wide rules.

Security & Privacy

Yes, we take data protection seriously:

  • Encryption: All data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Limited sharing: Only necessary information shared within groups
  • GDPR compliant: We comply with data protection regulations
  • User control: You control what information is visible
  • No selling: We never sell user data to third parties
  • Data deletion: Right to be forgotten upon request

If you suspect identity fraud:

  1. Immediate report: Contact support immediately
  2. Account freeze: We'll freeze the affected account
  3. Investigation: Our security team will investigate
  4. Law enforcement: We'll involve authorities if needed
  5. Resolution: Clear your name and restore account
  6. Prevention: Two-factor authentication available

Important: M-Pesewa does NOT handle financial transactions. All money transfers happen directly between users using their preferred payment methods (M-Pesa, bank transfer, etc.). This means:

  • No fund storage: We don't hold user money
  • Bank-level security: Use your bank's/M-Pesa's security
  • Direct control: You control your own money movements
  • No platform risk: We can't lose or misuse your funds
  • Transaction records: Keep your own payment receipts

Yes, you have the right to delete your account:

  • Account deletion: Available in account settings
  • Data retention: Some data kept for legal requirements (7 years)
  • Active loans: Must clear all loans before deletion
  • Ledger preservation: Loan records preserved for other parties
  • Anonymization: Personal data anonymized where possible
  • Export option: Download your data before deletion
