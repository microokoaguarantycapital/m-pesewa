1. What Are Cookies?

🍪

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, M-Pesewa) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies."

📋 Cookie Categories

Session Cookies

Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser

Persistent Cookies

Remain on your device until deleted or expired

First-Party Cookies

Set by M-Pesewa for platform functionality

Third-Party Cookies

Set by trusted partners for specific purposes

5. How to Control Cookies

A. Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Here are links to managing cookies for popular browsers:

Google Chrome Mozilla Firefox Safari Microsoft Edge

B. Platform Cookie Consent

When you first visit M-Pesewa, you will see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept All: Accept all cookies (essential, functional, analytics)
  • Essential Only: Only accept cookies necessary for platform operation
  • Customize: Choose which categories of cookies to accept
  • Reject All: Reject all non-essential cookies

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our platform.

C. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites you do not want to have your online activities tracked. At this time, we do not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals as there is no consistent industry standard for implementation.

⚠️ Consequences of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, please be aware that:

  • You may not be able to log in to your account
  • Your preferences may not be saved
  • Some platform features may not work correctly
  • Security features may be compromised
  • We may not be able to provide optimal support

🔧 Controlling Local Storage

To clear local storage and session storage:

  1. Open browser developer tools (F12)
  2. Go to the Application or Storage tab
  3. Find Local Storage or Session Storage for mpesewa.com
  4. Right-click and select "Clear" or delete specific items

Note: Clearing local storage will log you out and reset your preferences.
