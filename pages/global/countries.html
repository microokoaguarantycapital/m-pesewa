Country Rules & Isolation

Strict Country Isolation

No cross-country lending or borrowing is allowed. All transactions happen within your selected country.

Currency Lock

All transactions use the local currency of your selected country. No currency conversion is provided.

Group Country Lock

Groups are country-specific. You cannot invite members from different countries to the same group.

Permanent Selection

Country selection is permanent after registration. To change countries, you must create a new account.
