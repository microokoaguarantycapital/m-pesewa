How M-Pesewa Works

A step-by-step guide to Africa's most innovative emergency lending platform

📌 Platform Role (Very Important)

M-Pesewa is NOT a bank, NOT a lender, NOT a borrower, and does NOT hold user funds.
We are a technology platform that enables verified users to organize into groups and facilitate lending within trusted circles. All money transfers happen directly between users using their own chosen payment methods.

5-Step Process

1

Join or Create a Group

Start by joining an existing trusted group (family, church, professional network) or create your own. Groups are invitation/referral only and country-locked.

  • Minimum 5 members, maximum 1,000 per group
  • Each group has one Admin/Founder
  • Country badge displayed on all groups
  • No cross-country invitations allowed
2

Choose Your Role

👤 Borrower

  • No subscription fees
  • Can join up to 4 groups (with good rating)
  • Request emergency loans for specific needs
  • Can also be a lender (dual role)

💼 Lender

  • Subscription required (Basic, Premium, Super)
  • Lend only within your group
  • Create unlimited personal ledgers
  • Can also be a borrower (dual role)
3

Emergency Loan Request

Borrowers request loans for specific emergency categories:

⏰

7-Day Terms

All loans have 7-day repayment period

💰

10% Interest

Fixed interest rate for all loans

📋

20 Categories

Specific needs (fare, data, gas, medicine, etc.)

⚖️

5% Daily Penalty

After 7 days on outstanding balance

4

Ledger & Repayment System

When a lender approves a loan, the system automatically creates a ledger:

📊 Ledger Fields

  • Borrower name & contact
  • Two guarantor/referrer contacts
  • Loan category & amount
  • Dates (borrowed, due, overdue)
  • Interest (10%) & Penalties (5% daily)
  • Status: Active / Cleared

🔄 Repayment Rules

  • Partial daily repayments allowed
  • Default after 2 months of non-payment
  • Blacklist badge for defaulters
  • 5-star rating system for borrowers
  • Admin can override ledgers if needed
5

Reputation & Community Building

Your reputation determines your access and opportunities:

⭐ Good Reputation

  • Access to more groups (up to 4)
  • Higher borrowing limits
  • Faster loan approvals
  • Better lending terms

⚠️ Blacklist Consequences

  • Cannot borrow from any group
  • Cannot join new groups
  • Visible platform-wide badge
  • Removal only by Admin after full repayment

Strict Platform Rules (Non-Negotiable)

🌍

Country Isolation

No cross-country lending or borrowing. Each country operates independently with local currency.

👥

Group Isolation

Lenders can only lend within their group. No cross-group lending allowed.

👤

Borrower Limits

Maximum of 4 groups per borrower, only with good rating (5-star system).

💰

Subscription Enforcement

Lenders are blocked when subscription expires (28th of each month).

👑

Admin Supremacy

Platform Admin can override any blacklist or ledger entry if needed.

🛡️

Referral-Only Model

All users join via referral from someone who knows them well (2 referrers required).

Ready to Get Started?

👤

As a Borrower

  1. Get invited to a group by someone you know
  2. Register as a borrower (no subscription needed)
  3. Browse emergency categories and request a loan
  4. Receive offers from lenders in your group
  5. Get funds directly to your phone/bank
  6. Repay within 7 days with 10% interest
Start Borrowing
💼

As a Lender

  1. Create or join a group as Admin/Founder
  2. Register as a lender and choose subscription tier
  3. Pay subscription fee via M-pesewa Till
  4. Browse loan requests in your group
  5. Approve loans and send funds directly
  6. Track repayments and rate borrowers
Start Lending
