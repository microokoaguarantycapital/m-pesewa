1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

Category Examples Purpose
Identity Information Full name, national ID, date of birth Verification, KYC compliance
Contact Information Phone number, email, physical address Communication, account recovery
Financial Information Subscription payment details Processing payments, billing
Profile Information Username, password, preferences Account management, personalization
Referral Information Guarantor names and contacts Trust verification, risk assessment

B. Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent
  • Location Data: Country, city (for country isolation enforcement)
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: Session data, preferences

C. Information from Third Parties

  • Credit Reference Bureaus: For Super and LOL tier lenders
  • Social Media: When using Google login option
  • Group Members: Referral and reputation information

⚖️ Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • Contract: To provide the services you requested
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with financial regulations
  • Legitimate Interest: To improve services and prevent fraud
  • Consent: For marketing communications (opt-in only)

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

⚠️ Limited Sharing Principle

We minimize data sharing. Your information is only shared when necessary for platform operation or required by law.

A. Within Your Trusted Circle

  • Group Members: Basic profile information (name, rating, group status)
  • Lenders in Your Group: Loan request details, repayment history
  • Group Admin: Full profile access for moderation purposes
  • Borrowers You Lend To: Your lender profile and terms

B. With Service Providers

  • Payment Processors: For subscription payments only
  • Hosting Providers: Secure data storage and backup
  • Analytics Services: Anonymous usage statistics
  • Communication Tools: For notifications and support

C. Legal & Safety Disclosures

  • Law Enforcement: When required by court order or legal process
  • Regulatory Authorities: For compliance with financial regulations
  • Fraud Prevention: To investigate and prevent illegal activities
  • Emergency Situations: To protect vital interests of users

D. Business Transfers

In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may be transferred. We will notify you and provide choices regarding your data.

4. Data Security

Our Security Measures

🔐 Encryption
End-to-end encryption for all sensitive data
🛡️ Access Controls
Role-based access, multi-factor authentication
📊 Regular Audits
Security assessments and penetration testing
🚨 Incident Response
24/7 monitoring and rapid response team

👤 Your Security Responsibilities

  • Keep your password confidential and use strong passwords
  • Enable two-factor authentication when available
  • Log out after each session, especially on shared devices
  • Report suspicious activity immediately to support
  • Keep your device software and antivirus updated

🚨 Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you within 72 hours as required by law, and provide guidance on protective measures you should take.

5. Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Active Account Data While account is active + 7 years Legal and tax requirements
Loan Ledgers Permanent (anonymized after 7 years) Financial record keeping
Blacklist Records 7 years after clearance Risk management
Website Logs 1 year Security monitoring
Marketing Data Until consent withdrawal User preference

Note: After retention periods expire, data is either deleted or anonymized. Anonymized data may be used for statistical analysis and platform improvement.

6. Your Rights & Choices

👁️

Right to Access

Request a copy of your personal data in machine-readable format

✏️

Right to Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data

🗑️

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)

⏸️

Right to Restriction

Limit processing of your data in certain circumstances

📤

Right to Portability

Transfer your data to another service provider

👎

Right to Object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests

📝 How to Exercise Your Rights

  1. Submit a request through your account settings dashboard
  2. Email privacy@mpesewa.com with "Data Rights Request" in subject
  3. Include your username and verification details
  4. We will respond within 30 days (may extend for complex requests)
  5. No fee for standard requests (excessive requests may incur fees)

✅ Consent Management

You can manage your consent preferences at any time:

  • Marketing communications: Opt-in/out in account settings
  • Cookies: Manage through browser settings or our cookie banner
  • Location data: Control through device settings
  • Push notifications: Configure in app settings

7. International Data Transfers

🌍 Data Location & Transfers

Primary Storage: Your data is stored on servers located in your country of registration whenever possible to comply with data localization laws.

Cross-Border Transfers: Some data may be transferred to:

  • Our regional headquarters for support and analysis
  • Service providers in countries with adequate data protection
  • Legal authorities when required by international law

All international transfers comply with GDPR Chapter V requirements and use appropriate safeguards like Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children's Privacy

🚫 Age Restriction

M-Pesewa is not intended for children under 18 years of age (or the legal age of majority in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we make material changes:

  • We will notify you via email and platform notification
  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top
  • We will provide a summary of changes for transparency
  • Continued use after changes means acceptance of new policy

Archive: Previous versions of this policy are available upon request.
