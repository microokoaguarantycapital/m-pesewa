Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter your email or phone number, and we'll send you a reset link. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder.

Why is my subscription not working?

Subscriptions expire on the 28th of each month. If your subscription isn't working, check if it has expired and make a new payment. Payments may take up to 15 minutes to process.

How do I appeal a blacklist?

Blacklist appeals must be submitted to the Platform Admin. Contact support with your username and details of why you should be removed from the blacklist.

Can I change my country after registration?

No, country selection is permanent due to regulatory requirements. To use M-Pesewa in another country, you must create a new account with that country's credentials.
