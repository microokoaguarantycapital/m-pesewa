Subscription Rules & Terms

Platform Rules

  • Subscription renews automatically on 28th of each month
  • Failed payment blocks lending access immediately
  • No cross-group lending - stay within your groups
  • No cross-country lending - country isolation enforced
  • Borrowers pay NO fees - ever
  • Platform does NOT hold money at any time

Lender Responsibilities

  • You choose who to lend to within your groups
  • You set interest rates (max 10% for 7 days)
  • You manage repayments and update ledgers
  • You enforce repayment within your groups
  • You rate borrowers after loan completion
  • You assume lending risks - no platform guarantees
⚠️
Important Disclaimer
M-PESEWA is a technology coordination platform only. We do not guarantee loan repayment, participate in debt collection, or hold money. All lending risks are borne by lenders. Default after 2 months leads to global blacklisting of the borrower.
