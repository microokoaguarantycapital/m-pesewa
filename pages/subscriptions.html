Subscription Rules & Terms

Clear rules for a transparent lending ecosystem. Understanding these ensures smooth operations for everyone.

📋 Platform Rules

  • Automatic Renewal: All subscriptions renew automatically on the 28th of each month at 23:59 local time
  • Payment Failure: Failed payment blocks lending access immediately until payment is confirmed
  • Group Isolation: No cross-group lending - you can only lend to members of groups you belong to
  • Country Isolation: No cross-country lending - each country operates as an independent ecosystem
  • Borrower Freedom: Borrowers pay NO fees - ever. They use the platform for free
  • No Custody: Platform does NOT hold money at any time. All transactions are direct between users
  • Weekly Reset: Weekly lending limits reset every Monday at 00:00 local time
  • Plan Changes: You can upgrade anytime; downgrades take effect at next billing cycle

👨‍⚖️ Lender Responsibilities

  • Borrower Selection: You choose who to lend to within your trusted groups
  • Interest Rates: You set interest rates (maximum 10% for standard 7-day loans)
  • Ledger Management: You manage repayments and update ledgers manually
  • Repayment Enforcement: You enforce repayment within your groups through social pressure
  • Borrower Rating: You rate borrowers after loan completion (1-5 stars)
  • Risk Assumption: You assume all lending risks - no platform guarantees on repayment
  • Default Reporting: You report defaults after 2 months for blacklisting
  • Group Dynamics: You maintain healthy group relationships for sustainable lending
⚠️
Important Platform Disclaimer

M-PESEWA is a technology coordination platform only. We provide the tools and framework for trusted lending but do not participate in financial transactions, guarantee loan repayment, or hold money.

All loans are private agreements between users. Default after 2 months leads to global blacklisting of the borrower. Debt collection is the responsibility of lenders, though we provide access to vetted debt collectors as a separate service.

Remember: Lend only to people you trust within your groups. Start small, build relationships, and scale gradually as you gain experience and confidence.

🌍 Hierarchy Enforcement Reminder

Global → Country → Groups → Lenders → Borrowers (Ledgers)

This hierarchy is strictly enforced. Your subscription gives you access to lend within your selected country, to borrowers in groups you belong to. No exceptions. This isolation protects you from cross-group and cross-country risks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about M-PESEWA subscriptions answered

When does my subscription renew? ▼

All subscriptions renew automatically on the 28th of each month at 23:59 (local time in your country). You'll receive a reminder email 3 days before renewal. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time, but your lending access will be blocked if your subscription expires.

What happens if I exceed my weekly limit? ▼

If you reach your weekly lending limit, you cannot create new ledgers until the limit resets (every Monday at 00:00). Existing ledgers continue unaffected. You can upgrade your plan at any time to increase your limits, but the new limits take effect immediately.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing? ▼

Yes, you can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time. When switching to annual, you'll pay the annual price and your billing cycle changes to yearly. When switching to monthly, the change takes effect at your next annual renewal date. Pro-rated refunds are not provided.

What happens if a borrower defaults? ▼

If a borrower defaults (no repayment after 2 months), you should mark them as defaulted in their ledger. They are automatically added to the global blacklist and cannot borrow from any group on M-PESEWA. You can access our directory of vetted debt collectors, but debt collection is your responsibility. The platform provides tools but does not participate in recovery.

Can I have multiple subscriptions for different groups? ▼

No, each lender account can have only one active subscription. However, you can join multiple groups within your country (up to 4 as a borrower, unlimited as a lender) and lend to members across all your groups using your single subscription. All lending counts toward your single set of limits.

How do I cancel my subscription? ▼

You can cancel auto-renewal in your account settings. Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period. After cancellation, you'll retain read-only access to your ledgers but cannot create new loans. There are no refunds for unused time. To restart lending, you'll need to purchase a new subscription.
