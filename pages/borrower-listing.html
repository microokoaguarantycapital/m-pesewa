Important Notes for Lenders

👥

Group-Scoped Only

You can only see borrowers from groups you belong to. No cross-group visibility to maintain trust networks.

💰

One Lender Per Loan

Unlike other platforms, M-PESEWA allows only one lender per loan. You fund the entire amount or not at all.

🛡️

Trust-Based Assessment

Use your personal knowledge of borrowers. The platform provides data, but trust is your responsibility.

📅

7-Day Standard Term

Most emergency loans are for 7 days at 10% interest. Penalty of 5% daily applies after due date.

⚠️

Default Consequences

Default after 2 months leads to global blacklisting. Borrower cannot borrow from any group on the platform.

🌍

Country Isolation

You only see borrowers from your selected country. No cross-border lending to prevent currency and regulatory issues.

⚠️
Platform Disclaimer
M-PESEWA is a technology coordination platform only. We do not guarantee loan repayment, participate in debt collection, or hold money. All lending decisions and risks are yours. Lend only to people you trust within your groups.
