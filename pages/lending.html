How Lending Works

1️⃣

Browse Requests

View loan requests from borrowers in your groups only. No cross-group lending.

2️⃣

Fund Loans

Choose which requests to fund based on your assessment and available funds.

3️⃣

Track in Ledger

Each funded loan creates a ledger entry for tracking repayments and interest.

4️⃣

Disburse Funds

Send money directly to the borrower using your preferred method outside platform.

5️⃣

Receive Repayment

Borrower repays principal + 10% interest within 7 days directly to you.

6️⃣

Update Ledger

Mark loan as cleared in your ledger. Rate borrower for future reference.

⚠️

Important Lending Rules

• Maximum 1 active loan per borrower per group
• 10% interest for 7-day loans (5% daily penalty after due date)
• Default after 2 months = global blacklist
• Subscription required for lending (expires 28th monthly)
• M-PESEWA does not hold money - all transactions happen directly between users
