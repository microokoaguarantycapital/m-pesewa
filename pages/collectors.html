How to Use This Directory

For Lenders

  • Contact collectors only after loan is 60+ days overdue
  • Verify collector credentials independently
  • Agree on fees before engaging services
  • Maintain professional communication
  • Document all agreements in writing

For Borrowers

  • Collectors should respect your dignity and rights
  • Verify collector is legitimate before sharing information
  • Request written proof of authorization from lender
  • Know your rights under local debt collection laws
  • Report any harassment or unethical practices
📋

Legal & Ethical Guidelines

All debt collection must comply with local laws. Harassment, threats, or unethical practices are prohibited. Collectors must operate within legal frameworks of their respective countries. M-PESEWA reserves the right to remove collectors who violate ethical standards.
