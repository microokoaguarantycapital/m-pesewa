Côte d'Ivoire Rules & Regulations

Specific rules for Ivorian operations

🇨🇮

CFA Franc Only

All transactions in CFA Franc (XOF). No foreign currency operations.

🚫

No Cross-Border

Lending and borrowing strictly within Ivorian borders only.

👥

Ivorian Groups

Only groups with Ivorian residents can operate in this network.

🆔

Ivorian ID Required

Valid Ivorian ID required for all lenders and borrowers.

📱

Local Mobile Money

Disbursements via local mobile money systems (Wave, Orange Money).

⚖️

Ivorian Law

All agreements subject to Ivorian contract law.
