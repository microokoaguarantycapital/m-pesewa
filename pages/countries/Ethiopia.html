Ethiopia Rules & Regulations

Specific rules for Ethiopian operations

🇪🇹

Ethiopian Birr Only

All transactions in Ethiopian Birr (ETB). No foreign currency operations.

🚫

No Cross-Border

Lending and borrowing strictly within Ethiopian borders only.

👥

Ethiopian Groups

Only groups with Ethiopian residents can operate in this network.

🆔

Ethiopian ID Required

Valid Ethiopian ID required for all lenders and borrowers.

📱

Local Mobile Money

Disbursements via local mobile money systems (M-Birr, etc.).

⚖️

Ethiopian Law

All agreements subject to Ethiopian contract law.
