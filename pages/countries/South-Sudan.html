South Sudan Rules & Regulations

Specific rules for South Sudanese operations

🇸🇸

South Sudanese Pound Only

All transactions in South Sudanese Pound (SSP). No foreign currency operations.

🚫

No Cross-Border

Lending and borrowing strictly within South Sudanese borders only.

👥

South Sudanese Groups

Only groups with South Sudanese residents can operate in this network.

🆔

South Sudanese ID Required

Valid South Sudanese ID required for all lenders and borrowers.

📱

Local Mobile Money

Disbursements via local mobile money systems (MTN Mobile Money).

⚖️

South Sudanese Law

All agreements subject to South Sudanese contract law.
