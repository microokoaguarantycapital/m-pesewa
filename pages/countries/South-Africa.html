South Africa-Specific FAQs

Can I lend to someone in Namibia?

▼

No. M-PESEWA enforces strict country isolation. You can only lend to people within South African-registered groups.

What currency is used?

▼

All transactions in South Africa are conducted in South African Rand (ZAR).

Are there any FICA requirements?

▼

M-PESEWA follows basic KYC requirements but is not subject to full FICA as we don't hold money.
