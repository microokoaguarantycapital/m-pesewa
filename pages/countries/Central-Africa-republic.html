CAR Rules & Regulations

Specific rules for Central African Republic operations

🇨🇫

CFA Franc Only

All transactions in CFA Franc (XAF). No foreign currency operations.

🚫

No Cross-Border

Lending and borrowing strictly within CAR borders only.

👥

CAR Groups

Only groups with CAR residents can operate in this network.

🆔

CAR ID Required

Valid Central African ID required for all lenders and borrowers.

📱

Local Mobile Money

Disbursements via local mobile money systems (Moov, Orange).

⚖️

CAR Law

All agreements subject to Central African Republic contract law.
