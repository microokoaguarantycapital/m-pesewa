Tanzania-Specific FAQs

Can I lend to someone in Kenya?

▼

No. M-PESEWA enforces strict country isolation. You can only lend to people within Tanzanian-registered groups.

What currency is used?

▼

All transactions in Tanzania are conducted in Tanzanian Shilling (TZS).

Can I join groups in other countries?

▼

No. Once you register in Tanzania, you can only participate in Tanzanian groups.
