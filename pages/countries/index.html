Why Country Isolation?

M-PESEWA enforces strict country isolation to comply with local financial regulations, prevent cross-border money movement issues, and build trust within local communities. This ensures all lending happens between people who share cultural context and local trust networks.

Important: Once you select a country, you cannot switch to another country later.
