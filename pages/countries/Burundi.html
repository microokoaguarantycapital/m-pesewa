Burundi-Specific FAQs

Can I lend to someone in Rwanda?

▼

No. M-PESEWA enforces strict country isolation. You can only lend to people within Burundian-registered groups.

What currency is used?

▼

All transactions in Burundi are conducted in Burundian Franc (BIF).

Can I join groups in Tanzania?

▼

No. Once you register in Burundi, you can only participate in Burundian groups.
