Ghana-Specific FAQs

▼

No. M-PESEWA enforces strict country isolation. You can only lend to people within Ghanaian-registered groups.

What currency is used?

▼

All transactions in Ghana are conducted in Ghanaian Cedi (GHS).

Can I use Ghana Card for verification?

▼

Yes, Ghana Card is the primary identification document for Ghanaian users.
