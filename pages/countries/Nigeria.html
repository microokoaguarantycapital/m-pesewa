Nigeria-Specific FAQs

Can I lend to someone in Ghana?

▼

No. M-PESEWA enforces strict country isolation. You can only lend to people within Nigerian-registered groups.

What currency is used?

▼

All transactions in Nigeria are conducted in Nigerian Naira (NGN).

Can I use BVN for verification?

▼

Yes, Nigerian users can use BVN or NIN for identity verification.
