Somalia Rules & Regulations

Specific rules for Somali operations

🇸🇴

Somali Shilling Only

All transactions in Somali Shilling (SOS). No foreign currency operations.

🚫

No Cross-Border

Lending and borrowing strictly within Somali borders only.

👥

Somali Groups

Only groups with Somali residents can operate in this network.

🆔

Somali ID Required

Valid Somali ID required for all lenders and borrowers.

📱

Local Mobile Money

Disbursements via local mobile money systems (Zaad, EVC Plus).

⚖️

Somali Law

All agreements subject to Somali contract law.
