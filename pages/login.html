Platform Rules & Security

Understanding these rules ensures safe and effective use of M-PESEWA

Login & Account Security

  • One account per person - multiple accounts are prohibited
  • Account is locked to your selected country - cannot be changed
  • Strong passwords required (8+ characters, mixed case, numbers)
  • Regular password updates recommended every 90 days
  • Two-factor authentication available for enhanced security
  • Suspicious activity is monitored and may trigger verification

Platform Governance

  • No cross-country lending or borrowing - strict isolation
  • Group-based trust - you can only interact within your groups
  • Borrower limit: maximum 4 groups (with good rating)
  • Lenders require active subscription to operate
  • Default > 2 months leads to global blacklisting
  • All disputes resolved within groups first
⚠️
Critical Platform Disclaimer

M-PESEWA is a technology coordination platform only. We do not hold money, participate in financial transactions, or guarantee loan repayment. All loans are private agreements between users. Transactions occur outside the platform using your preferred payment methods.
