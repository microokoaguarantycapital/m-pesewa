About M-PESEWA

Emergency loans from people who know you. Inside trusted groups. Inside your country.

Our Mission

M-PESEWA exists to solve emergency consumer pain points by enabling friends to help friends, creating income opportunities for lenders, putting idle money to productive use, reducing reliance on predatory lending, and building trust-based financial ecosystems across Africa.

What is M-PESEWA?

💡

Our Vision

To be Africa's most trusted platform for emergency micro-lending within communities.

🎯

Our Purpose

To provide immediate financial relief for real emergencies through trusted relationships.

What We Are NOT

🏦

Not a Bank

We don't take deposits or give loans from our own capital.

💰

Not a Lender

We don't lend money. We connect trusted people who do.

👤

Not a Borrower

We don't borrow money. We facilitate connections.

🔒

Not a Custodian

We never hold money. All transactions happen directly.

Core Philosophy

The principles that guide everything we do

🤝

Trust-Based

Lending happens only within established trust circles where people know each other.

🌍

Country-Focused

Each country operates independently with its own currency and regulations.

🛡️

Safety First

Strong reputation systems and blacklist enforcement protect all participants.

🚨

Emergency Focus

Designed for real emergency needs, not luxury or investment.

📊

Transparent

Clear terms, no hidden fees, and complete ledger transparency.

⚖️

Fair & Ethical

Interest rates agreed willingly between trusted parties.

Enforced Rules

  • ❌ No cross-country lending or borrowing
  • ❌ No cross-group lending
  • ✅ Borrower: max 4 groups (good rating required)
  • ❌ Lenders blocked when subscription expires (28th monthly)
  • ❌ Borrower with active loan cannot request another in same group
  • ❌ Default > 2 months → platform-wide blacklist
  • ✅ Admin can override ledgers & blacklist

Why This Structure?

  • Prevents fraud across borders
  • Builds trust through existing relationships
  • Reduces risk through group accountability
  • Ensures compliance with local regulations
  • Creates sustainable financial ecosystems
  • Protects both lenders and borrowers
