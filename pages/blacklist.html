How Blacklisting Works

Day 1-7

Loan Active

Normal loan period with 10% interest.

Day 8+

Overdue Period

5% daily penalty on outstanding balance. Group enforcement begins.

Day 60+

Automatic Blacklist

Borrower added to global blacklist. Cannot borrow or join new groups.

After Repayment

Removal Process

Full repayment + Admin approval required for removal from blacklist.

Consequences of Blacklisting

❌
Platform-Wide Blocking
Blacklisted users cannot borrow anywhere on M-PESEWA.
🚫
Group Restrictions
Cannot join new groups. Existing groups may expel blacklisted members.
📢
Public Registry
Name appears in this public registry visible to all lenders.
✅
Removal Conditions
Full repayment of principal + interest + penalties + Admin approval required.
