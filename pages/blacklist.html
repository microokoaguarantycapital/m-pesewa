How Blacklisting Works

Strict process triggered after 2 months of non-payment

1

Days 1-7: Normal Loan Period

Standard 7-day loan term with 10% interest rate. Repayment expected within this period.

2

Days 8-59: Overdue Period

5% daily penalty on outstanding balance. Group enforcement and reminders activated.

3

Day 60: Automatic Blacklist Trigger

System automatically adds borrower to blacklist. Lender must verify and tick blacklist badge.

4

Day 61+: Global Platform Block

Borrower cannot borrow anywhere on M-PESEWA. Cannot join new groups. Appears in public registry.

5

After Full Repayment: Admin Review

Only Platform Admin can remove blacklist badge after verifying full repayment (principal + interest + penalties).

Consequences of Blacklisting

❌

Platform-Wide Blocking

Blacklisted users cannot borrow anywhere on M-PESEWA. All loan requests are automatically rejected.

🚫

Group Restrictions

Cannot join new groups. Existing groups receive notifications and may vote to expel blacklisted members.

📢

Public Registry Listing

Name appears in country-specific public registry visible to all lenders in that country.

🏷️

Blacklist Badge System

Each lender who experiences default can tick the blacklist badge. Badge shows count of confirming lenders.

Removal Conditions

✅

Full Repayment Required

Principal + 10% interest + accumulated penalties must be paid in full to all affected lenders.

👑

Admin Approval Only

Only Platform Admin can uncheck blacklist badges after verifying repayment with all lenders.

🔄

Reputation Reset

After removal, borrower rating is reset to neutral. Previous default remains in permanent record.

⏰

Time Restrictions

Repeated defaults lead to permanent blacklisting with no possibility of removal.
