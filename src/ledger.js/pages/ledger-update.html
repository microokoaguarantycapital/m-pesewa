Ledger Update Rules

  • Append-Only: You cannot modify or delete existing ledger entries
  • Audit Trail: All entries are permanently recorded with timestamp and user ID
  • Interest: 10% interest is automatically calculated weekly
  • Penalty: 5% daily penalty applies after 7 days of overdue
  • Default: Loans enter default state after 2 months (60 days)
  • Admin Oversight: Platform administrators can review and validate all entries
