Closure Impact & Consequences

Positive Impacts
  • Improves borrower credit rating
  • Increases lender reputation score
  • Boosts group success rate
  • Clears space for new loans
  • Provides closure for both parties
Important Considerations
  • No further modifications allowed
  • Ledger becomes read-only
  • Cannot be reopened (except by admin)
  • Affects statistical calculations
  • Permanent audit record created
Post-Closure Actions
  • Borrower can apply for new loans
  • Lender can lend to other borrowers
  • Group can see completed loan in history
  • Platform updates success metrics
  • Tax documents may be generated

Legal & Compliance Requirements

Record Keeping Requirements

Closed ledgers must be maintained for a minimum of 7 years as per financial regulations.

Tax Implications

Interest earned may be subject to taxation. Lenders are responsible for declaring income.

Dispute Resolution

Once closed, disputes can only be reopened by platform administrators with proper justification.

Data Privacy

Closed ledger data remains subject to data protection laws and privacy policies.
