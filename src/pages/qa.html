Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about M-Pesewa lending, borrowing, groups, and subscriptions.

What is M‑Pesewa?

M‑Pesewa is an emergency micro‑lending platform built around trusted social groups. It enables individuals within the same country and group to lend and borrow short‑term consumption loans. All monetary transactions occur outside the platform (via M‑Pesa, bank, etc.). M‑Pesewa manages trust, structure, reputation, and visibility, not funds.

Who can join?

Anyone in the 13 supported African countries (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, Somalia, South Sudan, Ethiopia, DRC, Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana) can join as a borrower or lender. Borrowers must be invited or referred by existing group members. Lenders must pay a subscription fee.

How do loans work?

  • Maximum repayment period: 7 days
  • Interest: 10% per week (fixed)
  • Daily partial repayments allowed
  • Penalty after 7 days: 5% daily on outstanding balance
  • Default after 2 months → blacklist
  • One active loan per group per borrower at a time

Note: Subscriptions expire on the 28th of each month. Borrowers pay no subscription fees (except in higher tiers where a small borrower fee may apply for larger loans).

How are groups formed?

Groups are created by a founder/admin within a country. Minimum 5 members, maximum 1,000. Groups are invitation‑ or referral‑only and are country‑locked (no cross‑country members). Each group has one admin/founder who moderates members and ensures compliance.

What is the blacklist system?

Borrowers who default for more than 2 months are blacklisted. The blacklist badge is visible across all groups and blocks new borrowing and group joining. Removal is only by Platform Admin after full repayment (principal + interest + penalties).

Can I be both a borrower and a lender?

Yes, but you must maintain separate profiles. You cannot switch roles within a group; you must log out and register/login again for the other role. Each role has its own dashboard and permissions.

How does the loan shopping cart work?

Borrowers select a loan category (e.g., Transport, Food) and add it to their group’s cart. The cart is visible to all lenders in that group. Lenders choose which borrowers to fund based on rating, exposure, and blacklist status. Borrowers cannot choose lenders.
