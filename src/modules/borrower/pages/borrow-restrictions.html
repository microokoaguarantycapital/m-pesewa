🚨 Steps to Remove Restrictions

1

Clear Outstanding Balances

Repay any overdue loans including principal, interest, and penalties. This is the first step to removing borrowing restrictions.

2

Contact Platform Admin

If blacklisted, contact the platform admin after full repayment for manual review and removal from blacklist.

3

Improve Reputation Score

Maintain timely repayments on any new loans to improve your rating back to 3.5+ stars.

4

Request Group Re-Entry

Once restrictions are lifted, request to re-join groups or join new groups with good standing.
