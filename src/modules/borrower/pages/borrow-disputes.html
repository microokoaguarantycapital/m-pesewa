Dispute Resolution Guide

ℹ️ This guide explains how disputes work on M-Pesewa and what you can expect during the process.

📋 When to File a Dispute

You should file a dispute if you encounter any of the following issues:

  • The loan amount disbursed is different from what was agreed
  • The lender changed terms after disbursement
  • You were charged incorrect interest or penalties
  • The lender is harassing or threatening you
  • There's a mistake in the loan ledger

⏱️ Dispute Timeline

Filing Deadline
3 days after disbursement
Initial Response
24-48 hours
Resolution Time
3-7 business days

📝 What to Include in Your Dispute

For the best chance of resolution, include:

  • Clear description of the issue
  • Dates and times of relevant events
  • Exact amounts involved
  • Screenshots of conversations or agreements
  • Names of people involved
  • Your desired resolution

⚖️ Possible Outcomes

In Your Favor
Loan terms corrected, refund issued, or loan cancelled
Compromise
Partial resolution acceptable to both parties
Not in Your Favor
Dispute rejected if insufficient evidence or invalid claim
⚠️ False Disputes: Filing false or malicious disputes may result in:
  • Account suspension
  • Blacklisting from the platform
  • Legal action in severe cases
