About the Public Blacklist Registry

This registry serves multiple purposes:

  • Transparency: Builds trust by showing platform enforcement
  • Accountability: Encourages responsible borrowing and lending
  • Deterrence: Discourages defaults through public visibility
  • Verification: Allows lenders to check borrower history

Note: Personal contact information is never displayed publicly. Only user IDs and relevant financial details are shown.
