Frequently Asked Questions

When does my subscription expire?

All M-Pesewa subscriptions expire on the 28th of every month, regardless of when you subscribed. This standardization helps with administration across all 12 African countries we serve.

What happens if I don't renew by the 28th?

If your subscription expires, you enter a 7-day grace period. During this time, you can still view your portfolio but cannot create new loans. After the grace period, your account becomes read-only until you renew.

Can I switch between subscription tiers?

Yes, you can upgrade your tier at any time. The upgrade cost is prorated based on the remaining days in your current billing period. Downgrades are only allowed at the end of your billing period.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. M-Pesewa charges only subscription fees to lenders. There are no transaction fees, no commission on loans, and no fees for borrowers. The subscription fee is our only source of revenue.

How do payments work across different countries?

Subscriptions are priced and paid in your local currency. We support all major local payment methods in each country (M-Pesa, MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, bank transfers, etc.).

What is the "Lender of Lenders" tier?

This is our enterprise tier for professional lenders and institutions. It allows you to set custom interest rates and repayment periods (minimum 1 month). Perfect for microfinance institutions and professional lending groups.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

Subscription fees are non-refundable as per our Terms & Conditions. However, we offer a 3-day cooling-off period for new subscribers where you can cancel without penalty if you haven't made any loans.
