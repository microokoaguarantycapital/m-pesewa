Dispute Resolution Guidelines

Direct Resolution

Parties should first attempt to resolve the dispute directly through communication.

  • Share evidence and documentation
  • Discuss openly and respectfully
  • Seek mutual understanding

Group Mediation

If direct resolution fails, involve group members or admin for mediation.

  • Group Admin can mediate
  • Two impartial members can assist
  • Document the mediation process

Platform Escalation

For unresolved disputes, escalate to platform administrators.

  • Submit all evidence
  • Platform Admin makes final decision
  • Decision is binding for platform purposes

Legal Recourse

If platform resolution is unsatisfactory, parties may pursue legal action.

  • Platform provides dispute records
  • Parties are responsible for legal costs
  • Platform does not participate in court proceedings

Legal Implications of Disputes

Warning: False or malicious disputes may result in:

  • Immediate suspension of platform access
  • Permanent blacklisting from all groups
  • Legal action for defamation or harassment
  • Financial penalties for abuse of process

Only raise legitimate disputes with supporting evidence. The dispute system is for genuine disagreements, not harassment.
