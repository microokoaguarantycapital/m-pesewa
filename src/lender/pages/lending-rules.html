Lending Rules

Mandatory rules for all lenders

  • ✅
    Group Isolation: You can only lend to borrowers within your own group(s).
  • ✅
    Subscription Requirement: Active subscription is mandatory for lending.
  • ✅
    Ledger Creation: Each approved loan automatically creates a ledger entry.
  • ✅
    Manual Disbursement: All money transfers happen outside the platform.
  • ✅
    Repayment Tracking: You must manually update repayment status in the ledger.
  • ✅
    Borrower Rating: Rate borrowers using 5-star system after loan completion.
  • ✅
    Category Selection: Choose specific categories you're willing to lend in.
⚠️
CRITICAL REMINDER:

Your subscription expires on the 28th of every month. After expiry, your lending access will be blocked until renewal.

Loan Terms & Conditions

Standard terms for all loans

Repayment Period

7 days maximum
All loans must be repaid within 7 days of disbursement.

Interest Rate

10% flat rate
Applied on the principal amount for 7 days.

Penalty

5% daily
Applied daily after 7 days on outstanding balance.

Default

After 2 months
Loan is considered in default, borrower gets blacklisted.

Platform Role & Liability

What M-Pesewa does and doesn't do

✅ M-Pesewa DOES:

  • Provide ledger tracking and management
  • Enforce group and country isolation
  • Manage subscription access control
  • Maintain reputation and blacklist system
  • Facilitate borrower-lender connections

❌ M-Pesewa DOES NOT:

  • Hold or transfer user funds
  • Guarantee loan repayment
  • Manage debt collection
  • Set interest rates (platform suggests 10%)
  • Provide legal enforcement
