Bans & Blacklists Management

Manage member restrictions, blacklists, and monitor default risks within your group.

⚡ STRICT BLACKLIST RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

Blacklisting Triggers

  • Loan default after 2 months of non-payment
  • Consistent late repayments (3+ instances)
  • Fraudulent activity or misrepresentation
  • Violation of group trust rules

Blacklist Consequences

  • Cannot borrow from any group
  • Cannot join new groups
  • Visible blacklist badge platform-wide
  • Referrals from blacklisted users are flagged

Removal Process

  • Only by Platform Admin (not group admin)
  • Requires full repayment (principal + interest + penalties)
  • Appeal process with evidence
  • Reinstatement probation period
