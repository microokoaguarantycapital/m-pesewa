Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the appeal process take?

Appeals are typically reviewed within 3-5 business days. You will be notified of the decision via email and in-app notification.

What happens if my appeal is approved?

If approved, your blacklist status will be removed immediately, and all restrictions will be lifted. You can then resume normal platform activities.

What if I can't pay the full amount owed?

You can propose a repayment plan in your appeal. Admins may approve partial payments with a structured plan based on your situation.

How many appeals can I submit?

You can submit up to 3 appeals per calendar year. After 3 rejections, you must wait until the next year or contact support for exceptional circumstances.
